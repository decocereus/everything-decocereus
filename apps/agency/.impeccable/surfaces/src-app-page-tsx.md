## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a catalogue of working machines. Each workflow we build is drawn as a real isometric machine (Fig. 1, Fig. 2) with plain words beside it. It refuses the AI-agency default of a dark gradient hero, feature cards and a logo strip.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, black ink. Grey exists only as the figures' tone ramp and one secondary ink (#3d3d3d) for supporting text. No accent colour. Geist for all reading text at generous sizes. Geist Mono only for figure labels and readouts, where it is measurement. Hairline rules divide the page like a printed catalogue. Figures sit in anatomy plates (Fig n, title, hint, live readout, caption). The primary button is a solid black rectangle with white text. Links are underlined. Paper Shaders only as a faint paper grain, never as a colour effect.

STORY: The visitor understands that we put AI agents to work on their daily operations. They watch one workflow, the Morning Ops Report, run as a machine. They believe they stay in control, because the approval lever is theirs, and that a real engineer builds it. Then they book a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the wordmark on the left, four text links, and Book a call on the right. Below it, one text row: the headline large on the left, and on the right the subcopy, Book a call (primary) and See the example (link). Under that row, Fig. 1 spans the full content width as the dominant element, with its top edge and machine visible without scrolling at 1440 x 900; the readout sits just below the fold there (a full-width drawing at this aspect needs ~800px), and is visible at 390 wide and on taller desktops.

FORM: user-pinned direction, outside the roll. The user, verbatim: "let's start fresh and make a minimal fresh site design with isometric graphs or like with anatomy skill, and yeah, let's keep the color scheme simple" (black and white only). seed: none (user-pinned). Build path this session: code, chosen by the user for this round ("Prototype in code"); the recorded project default in .impeccable/config.json is unchanged.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
